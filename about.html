About Us

Hyderabad Home Tutors · Hyderabad, Sindh, Pakistan

What we do

Hyderabad Home Tutors is a home-tuition platform for Hyderabad, Sindh. We help parents find a tutor for their child by subject, class and area — and help tutors in Hyderabad find students near them.

How it works

  • Parents send a free request from the homepage, telling us the subject, class, area and preferred timing.
  • Tutors register on the site and apply with their subjects, classes and areas.
  • Verification: every tutor is checked before their listing goes live. Tutors share their CNIC number and CNIC photo for verification — these details are seen only by the admin and are never shown publicly. Full home addresses are also admin-only; the public listing shows the area only.
  • Matching: once a request is matched, the parent and tutor arrange the lessons directly. Tutors' monthly fees are agreed directly with the tutor and paid to the tutor.

Free to use

The platform is free for parents and for tutors. There is no charge to register, send a request or be listed.
