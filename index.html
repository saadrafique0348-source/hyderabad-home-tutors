Our 5-step verification

No tutor is listed until all five steps are complete.

1

Identity check

CNIC and a recent photograph are collected and matched against the tutor's application.

2

Qualification check

Degrees, transcripts and certificates are reviewed against the subjects the tutor wants to teach.

3

Interview

A short interview confirms teaching ability, communication and the classes the tutor can handle.

4

Trial lesson review

The tutor's first lesson feedback is reviewed before the profile stays public.

5

Ongoing parent reviews

Verified parents rate every tutor. Ratings are moderated, and tutors who fall short are removed.
