Trust & safety

Our 5-step verification

A tutor's listing goes live after steps 1–3 — the application, the CNIC identity check and the interview. Steps 4 and 5 then continue after listing, while the tutor teaches.

1

Identity check

The tutor's CNIC number and a photo of the front of their CNIC card are collected in the application and checked by the admin. The CNIC and full home address are kept private — only the admin can see them, never the public listing.

2

Qualification check

Degrees, transcripts and certificates are reviewed against the subjects the tutor wants to teach.

3

Interview

A short interview confirms teaching ability, communication and the classes the tutor can handle.

4

Trial lesson review

After listing, the tutor's first lesson feedback is reviewed — if it falls short, the listing is taken down.

5

Ongoing parent reviews

After listing, verified parents rate every tutor on an ongoing basis. Ratings are moderated, and tutors who fall short are removed.

Good to know

Questions parents ask us

Is it really free for parents?

Yes. Searching tutors, creating an account and sending a tuition request cost nothing, and there is no advance payment to the platform. You agree the monthly fee directly with your tutor and pay the tutor — Hyderabad Home Tutors is supported by advertising.

How are tutors verified?

A tutor is listed only after the first three steps: a CNIC identity check, a qualification review and a short interview. After listing, the tutor's trial lessons are reviewed and parents' reviews are moderated on an ongoing basis. A tutor's CNIC number, CNIC photo and full home address are kept private with the admin — the public listing only ever shows the tutor's base area.

Which areas of Hyderabad do you cover?

Verified tutors are listed across 14 areas: Qasimabad, Latifabad, Saddar, Hirabad, Auto Bhan Road, Gulistan-e-Sajjad, Naseem Nagar, Citizen Colony, Hyderabad Cantt, Pretabad / Phuleli, SITE Area, Jamshoro, Kotri and Tando Jam. Use the area chips or the map above to see the tutors nearest to your home first.

How do I track my tuition request?

Sign in and open “My Account”. Every request you send is listed there and moves through New → Contacted → Matched as our team and the tutor work on it, so you always know where things stand.

I am a tutor — how do I join?

Create a free tutor account, then complete the tutor application with your subjects, classes, qualification and CNIC details. Our team runs the 5-step verification, and your listing goes public only after approval. You can watch your application status any time in My Account.

Privacy Policy & Terms

In simple words: what information Hyderabad Home Tutors collects, who can see it, how it is used, and the rules for using this platform.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Accounts: your name, email address and phone number.
  • Tutors: your CNIC number and a photo of the front of your CNIC card, your qualification and experience, and your full home address.
  • Parents: in a tuition request — your phone number, the student's class and subject, your preferred timing, and the home address where the tuition will happen (the child's home location for lessons).
  • Reviews: the rating and text you submit, shown with your account name after moderation.

Who can see it

Public tutor listings show only a tutor's name, subjects, classes, qualification, experience, base area and monthly fee. CNIC numbers, CNIC photos and full home addresses are admin-only — they are stored separately from the public listing and are never shown on the public site. When a tuition request is matched, the tutor matched to that request can see the request details, including the tuition address, so the lessons can take place.

How we use it

We use this information only to verify tutors, match parents with tutors, run tuition requests, moderate reviews and operate the platform. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can ask for your information to be corrected or deleted at any time — message the platform owner through the chat on this site and we will act on it. This site shows advertising, and ad providers such as Google may use cookies as described in their own privacy policies.

Terms of Use

  • Hyderabad Home Tutors connects parents and students with home tutors in Hyderabad, Sindh. Monthly fees are agreed between the parent and the tutor and are paid directly to the tutor.
  • It is free to join and browse. If a platform fee for tutors is ever switched on, it will be a percentage of the tutor's first month's fee, and tutors will be told before it applies to them.
  • A tutor's listing goes live only after steps 1–3 of our verification — the application, the CNIC identity check and the interview. Trial-lesson feedback and parent reviews (steps 4–5) are checked after listing, and a tutor who falls short can be removed.
  • Give true information in your account, application, requests and reviews. Fake applications or reviews can be removed, and reviews are published only after moderation.
  • Keep your account password private. An email account counts as verified only after you open the verification link Firebase emails you.
  • We work to keep listings accurate, but we cannot guarantee a tutor's availability or a student's results.

Firestore security rules

The database was created with locked (deny-all) rules. Paste these rules in Firebase Console → Firestore Database → Rules → Publish to switch the platform on: approved tutors and reviews become publicly readable, parents and tutors can write only their own records, and only admins can approve anything or change settings.


          

            
            
            
          

          

          
Privacy cleanup of older records

          
Older records may still hold tutor phone/email in the public tutor listing, and parent contact details inside the request. This moves them into the private collections. It changes live data only when you press the button, and asks for confirmation first.

          

          

          

          
Firebase Storage security rules (CNIC photos)

          
CNIC card photos are stored under a per-tutor path (tutor_cnic/{tutorId}/…). Paste these rules in Firebase Console → Storage → Rules → Publish. A tutor can upload only into their own folder; only admins can view or download the photos. The public site never reads this storage.

          

          

            
            
          

          
Also enable Google sign-in: Firebase Console → Authentication → Sign-in method → Google → Enable, and add your live domain under Authentication → Settings → Authorized domains if it is not listed.

          
Email verification: built into Firebase Authentication — every new email/password account is sent Firebase's own verification link (sendEmailVerification), and the site treats Firebase Auth's emailVerified flag as the only verification state. Nothing about verification is stored in Firestore, and the rules below stop a user from writing any verified flag into their own users document. Google sign-in accounts are already verified by Google. You can optionally customise the verification email under Firebase Console → Authentication → Templates.

          
Phone-number sign-up (one manual step): Firebase Console → Authentication → Sign-in method → Phone → Enable, and make sure the live domain is under Authentication → Settings → Authorized domains. Firebase sends the 6-digit code by SMS text message only — it cannot send codes on WhatsApp. The free Spark plan has a small daily SMS quota, so expect “quota reached” errors if many people sign up by phone in one day.
