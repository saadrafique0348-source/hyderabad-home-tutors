Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026. Hyderabad Home Tutors is founded and managed by Saad Rafique, Hyderabad, Sindh, Pakistan. Contact: saadrafique0348@gmail.com.

Plain-language summary: we collect only what is needed to connect parents with tutors and to verify tutors. A tutor’s CNIC, CNIC photo and home address are visible only to our admin. A parent’s phone and home address are shared with a tutor only after we confirm a match.

1. What we collect

  • Parents / students: name, email or phone number, the student’s class and subject, preferred timing, a home address where lessons would take place, and messages you write.
  • Tutors: name, phone/WhatsApp, email, qualification, experience, subjects, classes, fee, base area, short bio, CNIC number, a photo of the front of the CNIC card, and full home address.
  • Reviews: rating and text, linked to your account and to the request you were matched on.
  • Location: only if you tap “Use my current location”. It is used in your browser to sort tutors by distance and is not stored by us.

2. Who can see what

  • Public: tutor name, base area (never the home address), subjects, classes, fee, qualification, experience, bio, and published reviews.
  • Admin only: CNIC number, CNIC photo, tutor home address, tutor phone and email, and all requests.
  • Parent contact details (name, phone, email, home address) are visible to you and our admin. They are shared with the requested tutor only after an admin marks the request Matched.

3. CNIC and document handling

  • Purpose: checking that a tutor is a real person before the tutor is listed. We do not use CNIC data for anything else, do not show it publicly and do not give it to parents.
  • Consent: tutors tick a consent box before submitting. You may withdraw consent by contacting us; your application will then be removed.
  • Storage and access: the CNIC number is stored in a private database record and the photo in private file storage, both on Google Firebase. Access is limited to site administrators by security rules; the uploader cannot download the photo again.
  • Deletion: we aim to delete the CNIC number and photo of a rejected or withdrawn application within 30 days, and of a tutor who leaves within 30 days of your request, unless the law requires us to keep them.

4. Children and parent / guardian consent

The service is for parents, guardians and tutors. A student under 18 should use it only with a parent or guardian, who is responsible for the account and agrees to this policy. We do not publish children’s names, photos or addresses.

5. Tutor verification policy

A tutor is listed after a CNIC identity check, a qualification review and a short interview. After listing, the first lesson is reviewed and parents matched with the tutor may leave moderated reviews. A listing can be removed if checks fail, information proves false, or reviews and complaints show a problem. Verification reduces risk but cannot guarantee a tutor’s conduct; please also use your own judgement and meet in a suitable setting.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Accounts and requests are kept while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and personal records at any time; we aim to do so within 30 days.
  • Published reviews may be anonymised or removed instead of kept with your name.
  • We may keep limited records where needed for safety, dispute handling or legal reasons.

7. Service providers and cookies

We use Google Firebase (sign-in, database, file storage), Google Fonts, OpenStreetMap map tiles, the Leaflet map library, and Google AdSense for advertising. These providers may receive your IP address and technical data when you load a page, and advertising providers may use cookies. You can manage Google ad personalisation in your Google account ad settings. If we offer the AI assistant, messages you type to it are processed by our AI provider; do not put CNICs or addresses into it.

8. Security

We use access rules so that private records can be read only by the people listed above, verified email for account actions, and HTTPS. No online system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting you we will tell you.

9. Your rights, questions and complaints

You may ask to see, correct or delete your information, withdraw consent, or make a complaint about how we handle it. Write to saadrafique0348@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 14 days and will explain our decision. This policy may change; the date above shows the latest version.
