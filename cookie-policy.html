1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small file that a website asks your browser to store on your device. When you visit the site again, your browser sends it back. Cookies help a site remember that you are signed in, remember your choices, and understand how visitors use the site. Similar storage in your browser (such as local storage) works in the same way.

2. Cookies we use

  • Strictly necessary — sign-in: Google Firebase keeps you signed in and protects your account. Without these, sign-in would not work. These are set only when you use an account feature.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics counts visits and shows which pages are popular, so we can improve the site. It does not identify you by name.
  • Advertising: when advertising is shown on this site, advertising providers such as Google may use cookies to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and measure ad performance. You can manage Google ad personalisation in your Google account ad settings.
  • Preferences: your browser may store small settings, such as keeping you signed in or remembering how you sorted tutors. These stay on your device.

3. Third-party cookies

Some cookies and similar storage come from the services this site uses: Google Firebase (sign-in and database), Google Analytics, Google Fonts, OpenStreetMap map tiles, the Leaflet map library, and advertising providers when ads are shown. These providers have their own privacy and cookie policies, and they may receive your IP address and technical data when a page loads.

4. How to control cookies

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and clear stored site data at any time. Every browser does this a little differently — look under Settings, then Privacy or Site Data.
  • You can manage Google ad personalisation in your Google account ad settings.
  • If you block strictly necessary cookies, sign-in and account features on this site may stop working. Browsing tutor listings does not need an account.
